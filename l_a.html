"ite A" mean "thing that greet A".

"i ite" mean "i greet" or "hello".

"i eti" mean "farewell" or "bye".

"d" is used for command. "t A B" mean "thing that cause A to be B".

"d t i ce lie" mean "make me have water" or "give me water".
